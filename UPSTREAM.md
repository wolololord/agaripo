# Upstream

AgarIPO started from an MIT-licensed Agar.io-style template by mbMayer. Its copyright
notice and licence are kept, unedited, in [UPSTREAM-LICENSE](UPSTREAM-LICENSE), and go
out with every deployed copy at `/UPSTREAM-LICENSE`.

None of the template's scripts, scenes, shaders or images ship today. What is left of it
is the project layout and the project file, both much changed since. Those parts stay
under the MIT licence. Everything else is under [LICENSE](LICENSE).
